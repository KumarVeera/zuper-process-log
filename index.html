Executive summary

The short version, every item linked to where the log records it. Quotes are from PROCESS_LOG.md; nothing here is new text about the work.

Key decisions

  1. Prompt 001 Build the crew capture loop first (shot checklist → guided camera → review → offline queue), then one office flow told through a single hero story: the gutter dispute.
  2. Prompt 002 Build order follows evidence: explicit first, then inferred, then the riskiest assumption. The brief asks for one office or customer view, so the customer view appears only as the end of the share flow.
  3. Prompt 005 The system shows facts, people judge: no automatic "fraud" or "safe" verdicts, no AI damage judgement. Missing-data warnings come before sharing, not after.
  4. Prompt 008 Only claim what is built. The quality check is real (sharpness + light measured on the photo). Demo photos and uploads are simulated and labelled as such. No GPS, verified or original-file badges.
  5. Prompt 022 Audit before building. Only the gaps were built, and each fix was re-tested with real files.
  6. Prompt 028 The review is the office's main task, so its action lives in the job card. It always shows the next step: Start, then Continue, then Mark reviewed.

Reflection on AI-assisted design

Undocumented I have not written my own final reflection yet. The points below are drawn only from what the log records, and each one links to its evidence.

  1. The AI over-reached on scope, and separating facts from guesses fixed it. The first breakdown listed 20 “functional requirements”, most of them the AI’s own ideas. Prompt 002 re-sorted them into explicit, inferred and assumed (Prompt 002, AI-errors table).
  2. Not every cut idea was the AI’s. The AI added a desktop intro panel and banners repeating the tapped quick action (removed in 015 and 020). The weather card and the Cards/Table switch came from the prompts themselves (016, 018) and were cut in 020 and 019.
  3. Testing in a real browser caught what reading the code didn’t. A regex cleanup that deleted CSS rules, a photo panel that shrank to nothing on short screens, keyboard focus lost after a decision. All were found at phone size in the browser preview (AI-errors table).
  4. An honesty rule set at the start held throughout. No GPS, “verified” or original-file claims unless they were built. The office view dropped the map and Verified badge from its own design because the crew app doesn’t record them (008, 024, thrown-away table).
  5. Not everything was finished. Connecting the crew app and office view through shared state was planned but not built (027), and 30 of 30 prompt files still have blank personal notes.
